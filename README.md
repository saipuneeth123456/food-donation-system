# 🍱 Food Donation System

A simple Java and MySQL application that helps manage surplus food donations and their distribution to people in need.

## 📌 Project Overview

The Food Donation System allows people with surplus food to donate it, while volunteers can collect the food and distribute it to people in need.

## 🔄 Workflow

Donor
↓
Donate Food
↓
Food Available
↓
Volunteer
↓
Collect Food
↓
Distribute to Needy People

## ✨ Features

### 👤 Donor

- Enter donor name
- Enter phone number
- Add food details
- Enter quantity
- Enter pickup location
- Enter food availability time

### 🤝 Volunteer

- View available food
- Collect food
- Distribute food to people in need
